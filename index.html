WHAT IT OFFERS

From drift signals to a monitoring decision.

↗

Multiple shift signals

Compare reference and incoming data using supported metrics such as KS, Wasserstein distance, PSI, and MMD.

◎

Historical risk estimation

Train a risk predictor on shift features paired with observed downstream prediction errors from historical batches.

⌁

Decision gate

Translate risk estimates into configurable ACCEPT, WATCH, or DEFER actions for further investigation.

HOW IT WORKS

A practical monitoring pipeline.

PreShift connects data-distribution monitoring with observed model performance. The risk predictor should be trained on representative historical batches where downstream error is measured.

RESEARCH TRANSPARENCY

Signals are not guarantees.

Shift metrics measure differences between datasets; they do not establish that prediction quality has degraded. Risk estimates depend on the quality and representativeness of historical training data and should not be assumed calibrated without validation. Evaluate on real, held-out batches before production use.
